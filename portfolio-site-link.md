# 🗺️ Linking to Freelancing Site

Adding a subtle, non-aggressive "Hire Me" call to action midway on your open-source project's homepage is **completely appropriate and standard practice**, and it will rarely turn people off if executed transparently. In the developer tools space, maintainer-for-hire signals are viewed as a badge of competence rather than a cheap sales pitch, provided the project itself is genuinely useful.

## ⚠️ Potential Risks & What Turns People Off

- **Aggressive Monetization Feel:** Pop-ups, flashing banners, or putting core documentation behind a paywall will instantly alienate open-source users.
- **Abandonware Anxiety:** If visitors think you built the tool *only* as a resume gimmick rather than a maintained utility, they won't adopt it.
- **Distraction:** A massive enterprise sales pitch on a lightweight utility page breaks user trust.

## ✅ Best Practices for Placement & Tone

- **Keep it Low-Key:** Place a clean, text-based callout box or a subtle banner between documentation sections or at the bottom of the "About" page rather than screaming at the top of the hero section.
- **Lead with the Tool:** Ensure the website's primary visual hierarchy is 95% about the code, documentation, and value of the agentic workflows.
- **Humanize the Copy:** Frame it around collaboration rather than corporate desperation. Something like: *"Looking for a platform engineer who knows Backstage inside and out? I built this tool and I'm currently open to new roles."* links directly to your profile.

## 💡 Execution Strategy for Your Job Hunt

- **Leverage the Newsletter/LinkedIn Push:** When you submit to platform engineering newsletters or message leads, position yourself as an expert creator sharing a tool you built to solve a real ecosystem gap.
- **Treat the Repo as a Portfolio:** Engineering managers hiring for Backstage roles care infinitely more about clean architecture and working code than a traditional resume.

## 🔗 Linking to Your About Page

- **It Works Perfectly:** Transitioning a user from an open-source tool to a professional portfolio is standard practice.
- **Optimize the Landing:** Ensure your freelancing website has a clear, prominent button leading directly to your **resume** or **LinkedIn profile**.
- **Match the Context:** Make sure the very top of your freelance "About" page clearly mentions that you are the creator of AI Crew Suite so the visitor knows they landed in the right place.

## 🗺️ Where to Place the CTA

- **Keep it on the Home Page:** This is the best spot for the mid-page text callout you originally planned.
- **Add to the Documentation Sidebar/Footer:** Do not put large CTA boxes inside your technical docs, as it disrupts reading. Instead, place a permanent, small link in the global **footer** or at the bottom of the documentation **sidebar** (e.g., *"Created by [Your Name] • Available for work"*).
- **Include in the GitHub Readme:** Add a small "Author" or "Consulting & Hire" section at the very bottom of your repository's `README.md` file.

## Primary Website CTA → Your Freelancing "About" Page

Link the main homepage callout box directly to your freelancing website's **About** page.

- **Why:** Engineering managers hiring for specialized Backstage roles want to see deep technical context. Your freelancing site contains your case studies and platform engineering articles, which instantly prove you are an expert, not just someone looking for any random job.
- **The Rule:** The "About" page must have a giant, impossible-to-miss button at the top that links directly to your LinkedIn or downloadable resume.
- Documentation Footer & GitHub README → LinkedIn Profile

Link the small, low-key links in your documentation sidebar, site footer, and GitHub repository directly to your **LinkedIn** profile.

- **Why:** When people are deep in code or documentation, a quick click to a LinkedIn profile feels low-friction and casual. It allows them to quickly scan your career history, see mutual connections, or send you a direct message without leaving their workflow ecosystem for a sales website.

## 🛠️ Summary of the Setup

| Placement Location                 | Link Target              | Context / Vibe                                  |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Main Homepage CTA Box**          | Freelance **About** Page | Deep dive into your case studies and expertise. |
| **Documentation Sidebar / Footer** | **LinkedIn** Profile     | Quick, professional peer-to-peer connection.    |
| **GitHub Repository README**       | **LinkedIn** Profile     | Standard developer portfolio backlink.          |
